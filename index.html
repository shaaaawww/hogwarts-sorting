06 / Synthesis

The patterns beneath the labels.

These are hypotheses from the conversation, not diagnostic claims or fixed truths about you.

I

You seek context before judgment.

Questioning, consulting and revisiting evidence appeared across school-like tasks, moral dilemmas and leadership situations.

II

Care increasingly comes with boundaries.

You offer second chances and preserve relationships, while also setting expectations and resisting unlimited accommodation.

III

You respect chosen structure, not blind obedience.

Order can be comfortable, yet you may bypass rules when they clash with self-knowledge, loyalty or what you judge to be right.

IV

Your imagination and standards pull in different directions.

Expansive goals coexist with a preference for order. Intrinsic curiosity and learned achievement pressure may both drive your work.

V

You can be collaborative without surrendering authority.

You'll invite dissent and seek understanding, but may ultimately choose the unpopular option if you think the evidence supports it.

VI

Agency may be the common thread.

You seem to value choosing your commitments, standards and ambitions—rather than having obligation, expectation or consensus decide for you.

The final question is less “Which House are you?” and more “Which values are yours—and which did you learn to survive, achieve or belong?”
